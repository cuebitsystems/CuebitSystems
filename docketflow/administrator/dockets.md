---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dockets"></Step>
  <Step title="Use filters">
    Client, contract, staff, dates, search, variation status — as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drafts">
    Draft dockets can be edited or deleted by their creator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submitted">
    Submitted dockets are final — open with **View** to see lines, plans, and signature.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also **Create Docket** from the Dockets page or from an Active contract's Contract Details.
