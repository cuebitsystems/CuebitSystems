---
title: "Rate Card"
description: "Variation minimums and staff rates."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rate Card">
    From **Contract Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set variation defaults">
    **Variation minimum quantity** and **variation item rate** if you use variation-category work items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add rate rows">
    Staff category × billing method → rate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save"></Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Rates on a work item are fixed when that work item is saved; changing the Rate Card later does not rewrite older work items.
</Note>
