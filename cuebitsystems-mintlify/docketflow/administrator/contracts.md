title: "Contracts"

---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Contracts"></Step>
  <Step title="Select New Contract">
    Complete required fields (project, client, billing type, dates as needed, retention if shown, status).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    **System Contract No** is assigned permanently. **Contract No** is saved from the form (prefilled from Next Contract No; you may edit it if it stays unique).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Contracts are grouped as **Active**, **Completed**, and **Inactive**. Click a row to open **Contract Details**.

## On Contract Details you can

- Edit contract fields and status.
- Open **Work Items**, **Rate Card**, **Staff Allocation**, **View Progress**, or **Create Docket** (when the contract is Active).
