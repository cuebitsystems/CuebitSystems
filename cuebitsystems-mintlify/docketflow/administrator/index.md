You manage the organisation's setup and commercial data. You see **Overview**,
**Contracts**, **Dockets**, and under **Settings**: **Users**, **Clients**,
**Organizational Profile**, and **Audit Log**.

## First-time setup checklist

Do these in order the first time you set up DocketFlow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Organizational Profile">
    Firm name, address, logo (optional), payment terms, GST treatment, retention defaults, and number prefixes for contracts and dockets. [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/organizational-profile)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Users">
    Create Supervisors and Field Technicians. [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/users)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clients">
    Add the customers you work for (and their contacts). [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/clients)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contracts">
    Create a New Contract for each job (status Active when people should work on it). [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/contracts)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate Card">
    Set variation minimums and staff rates for that contract (before or while adding work items). [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/rate-card)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work Items">
    Add the schedule of work (item numbers, descriptions, quantities, rates). [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/work-items)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Staff Allocation">
    Assign Field Technicians so they can create dockets on that contract. [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/staff-allocation)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dockets">
    Field staff create and submit dockets on site. [Details →](/docketflow/administrator/dockets)
  </Step>
</Steps>
