---
title: "Working Offline"
description: "Downloading contracts and syncing, for Supervisors."
---

1. **Download offline**
   On **Contracts** or **Contract Details**, use **Download offline** (or **Re-download offline**) for each contract you need on site.

2. **Work offline**
   Create or edit drafts and submit as usual; items may show **Queued** until sync finishes.

3. **Reconnect**
   When you are Online again, the app syncs. Caches you downloaded are kept for your browser profile (not trimmed by Field Technician assignment rules).
