---
title: "Day-to-day Workflow"
description: "The typical order of operations for a Supervisor."
---

1. **Open Contracts**
   Create a **New Contract** when needed, or open an existing one.

2. **Set up Contract Details**
   Configure **Rate Card**, **Work Items**, and **Staff Allocation** as required.

3. **Use View Progress**
   Check quantities and valuations.

4. **Download offline**
   Use on the Contracts list or Contract Details before site work without reliable network.

5. **Create or review dockets**
   Use **Create Docket** when you need to raise a docket yourself, or open **Dockets** to review drafts and submitted work across the team.

6. **Check Overview**
   A quick picture of active contracts and recent dockets / variations.
