## Sign in

1. Open the DocketFlow web app.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**.

If you received an invite or need to set a password for the first time,
follow the link in your email and choose a password when prompted.

## First-time prompts

You may be asked to:

- **Change your password** — required before you can use the rest of the app.
- **Complete your profile** — enter at least your full name under **Profile**.

## Online / Offline

In the top bar you will see **Online** or **Offline**.

- **Online** — you are connected; work saves to the server.
- **Offline** — Field Technicians can still work on contracts they downloaded
  earlier; dockets queue until you are back online.

## Sign out

Select **Sign out** in the top bar when you finish.
