title: "Users"
description: "Create/invite users, status badges, pending vs active vs inactive."
---

1. **Open Users**
   Under **Settings**.

2. **Create a user**
   With **Create User** (set a password) or tick **Send email invitation**.

3. **Choose a role**
   **Supervisor**, **Field Technician**, or **View Only** (you cannot create another Administrator from this screen).

4. **Optional: employee number**


## Status badges next to each team member

| Badge | Meaning | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** (amber) | Created but has never signed in | **Delete** to remove a typo account so you can recreate the same email. You cannot Deactivate Pending users. |
| **Active** (green) | Has signed in at least once and is allowed to use the app | **Deactivate** to revoke access (no Delete) |
| **Inactive** (red) | Deactivated | **Activate** to restore access. If they never signed in, **Delete** remains available. |

:::warning
Before **Delete** / **Cancel invite**, remove the person from **Staff Allocation** on any contracts if they were assigned.
:::

## Pending invites

Email invites not yet finished:

- **Set password**
- **Resend invite**
- **Cancel invite** (permanently removes the unused account)

**Expired** appears about 24 hours after the invite was sent — resend to refresh.

Email invitees appear only under **Pending invites** until they complete setup / first sign-in (not also under Team members).

For members who have signed in (not yourself, not the Administrator): **Set password** or **Send reset email** as needed.
