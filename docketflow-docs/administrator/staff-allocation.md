---
title: "Staff Allocation"
description: "Assign Field Technicians to a contract."
---

1. **Open Staff Allocation**
   From **Contract Details**.

2. **Tick Field Technicians**
   Who should work on this contract.

3. **Save**


:::note
Only users with the **Field Technician** role appear in this list. They must be assigned before they can create dockets for that contract (from their side of the app).
:::
