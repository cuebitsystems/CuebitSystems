---
title: "Organizational Profile"
description: "Firm details, numbering, and retention defaults."
---

1. **Open Organizational Profile**
   Under **Settings**.

2. **Fill in firm details**
   Used on documents and in the header welcome text.

3. **Set numbering**
   Contract and docket number prefixes / next numbers, if you want branded numbering.

4. **Review retention defaults**
   Used for claims.

5. **Save your changes**
