---
title: "Day-to-day Workflow"
description: "Creating and submitting a docket, for Field Technicians."
---

1. **Open My Contracts**
   Or Overview → Contracts Assigned to Me.

2. **Select a contract**
   Row or eye icon opens Contract Details.

3. **Optional: check Work Items**
   See agreed / completed / remaining quantities (no rates).

4. **Select Create Docket**

5. **Choose work items**
   The ones you completed, then **Continue**.

6. **Enter details**
   Worked quantities, notes, visit comments, and plans as needed.

7. **Sign off**
   Enter the client representative name, capture their signature, then **Save as Draft** if you are not finished, or **Sign Off / Submit** to finalise.


:::warning
Submitted dockets cannot be changed. Use **My Dockets** to find drafts (edit/delete) and submitted records (view only).
:::
