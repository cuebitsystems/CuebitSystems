---
title: "Working Offline"
description: "Downloading contracts and syncing, for Field Technicians."
---

1. **Download offline**
   On **My Contracts**, use **Download offline** (or **Re-download offline**) for each Active contract you need on site.

2. **Work offline**
   Create or edit drafts and submit as usual; items may show **Queued** until sync finishes.

3. **Reconnect**
   When you are Online again, the app syncs. If you see **Sync Error**, open the docket again when connected or contact your Administrator.


:::note
You only see your dockets — not other Field Technicians' work on the same contract.
:::
